# Exception Policy

Exceptions are explicit, narrow, time-bounded, and approved by the authority that owns the waived rule.

An exception records:

- rule and reason;
- affected Change and paths;
- risk and compensating control;
- evidence still required;
- expiry or removal condition;
- approver.

An exception may not silently authorize sensitive data egress, destructive action, automated business action, production deployment, cross-repository writes, test weakening, or completion without evidence.

Emergency work uses the smallest safe fix, an immediate regression test, and mandatory later specification, root-cause, verification, and archive.
