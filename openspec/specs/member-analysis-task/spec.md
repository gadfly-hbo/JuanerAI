protocol: execute_plan, read_result, question or pending-report explanation only.

writes exist. Actual payloads/tool responses are freshly projected from the pinned

## A expired/drifted grant — SC-08/09, CAP-05

immutable snapshot recheck protects execution even without a new selection.

## Second Validator correction: lexical boundaries and exact confirmation

F1/CAP05/SC10: sentence punctuation, quoting, Unicode compatibility characters and
percent encoding cannot turn recognizable filesystem material into safe text.
A relative file extension ends at a lexical boundary, not only at end of token.
Numeric division with optional whitespace, dates and ordinary business text remain
accepted byte-for-byte. Normalization is inspection-only. Reject before transport
or credential access; existing returned/history/adopted material consumers share
the same rule and other material negatives remain unchanged.

F4/SC11–12/UI §3.2: a human acceptance belongs to the exact task, owner, review
ID/version/hash/intent, source, fields and current report/Finding/plan identity.
Any changed head/content/source or local edit invalidates it at render and submit
admission; a saved callback from an old render cannot submit a new head or bypass
current admission. Preserve edits and explicit same-source continuation. Choice and
analysis require fresh exact acceptance; more-evidence retains its existing explicit
non-acceptance effect and cannot submit a stale/dirty/unknown head. No new IPC,
persistence, formal effect, resource, or user approval contract is introduced.

## Third Validator correction: complete slash expressions (F1)

CAP05/SC10 and product decisions §3.1: inspect a whole slash-connected expression
before exempting numeric division or dates. A numeric prefix never exempts an
absolute root or a later nonnumeric segment. Refuse rooted, home/dot-relative and
relative directory/file syntax, including hidden and extensionless names, without
depending on a suffix list. Sentence punctuation and encoded/compatibility forms
cannot hide that syntax. Preserve complete numeric ratios/dates (including spaces)
and ordinary Chinese business-label ratios. Ambiguous path-shaped ASCII expressions
stay local. Inspection must not rewrite accepted outbound text.

Existing actual Pi new/retained admission and Application supplement/comment,
history/report, returned/adopted material consumers use the same predicate. Refuse
before transport or credential access at admission; a refused issued response keeps
its charge and verified work but gains no publication/reuse effect. No schema,
authority, resource or formal-review contract changes. F2–F4 remain unchanged.

## Fourth Validator correction: business ratio operands (F1)

CAP05/SC10: a complete ratio may have spaced percent suffixes, numeric quantities
with Chinese units, and Chinese labels with parenthesized units. These selected
business expressions must reach actual Pi unchanged in new/retained/history and
reused material. Unit punctuation belongs to its operand, not a standalone path
root. Recognize whole operands while retaining every slash and checking the entire
connected expression: attached or adjacent roots, extra path segments, hidden
names and encoding still refuse before credential/transport access. No numeric
prefix or safe ratio may exempt surrounding prohibited material. Existing returned
material admission and all F2–F4 effects remain unchanged; no contract expansion.

## Fifth correction: operand whitespace invariance (F1)

Recognize a Chinese label and its balanced unit annotation before splitting slash
expressions. Optional ordinary/repeated spaces and supported normalized-width spaces
at label/annotation, annotation/unit and ratio-separator boundaries do not change
admission. Numeric/percent/unit operands retain the same whitespace invariance.
Accepted text is byte-identical. Recognizing a complete annotated ratio never
consumes adjacent roots, extra relative segments or encoded paths; all surrounding
material still passes the existing whole-expression checks. CAP05/SC10 apply;
no new data, runtime, persistence or authority contract.
