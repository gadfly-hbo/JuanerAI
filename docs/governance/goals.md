# Three-Objective Management

JuanerAI interprets three-objective management as one complete decision package for every material Change.

## Product Objective

State the user or business outcome and how the Decision Loop improves. This prevents platform work from becoming the product.

## Delivery Objective

State the exact observable capability, scope, acceptance criteria, evidence level, and rollback. This prevents activity from being mistaken for completion.

## Learning Objective

State the assumption being tested and what reusable product, domain, architecture, test, or governance knowledge should be retained. This prevents each Change from starting with no memory.

## Rule

The Product Objective has priority. Delivery must prove it. Learning may improve future work but cannot expand current scope.

If a Change has no credible Product Objective, classify it explicitly as risk reduction, enablement, or governance rather than inventing user value.
